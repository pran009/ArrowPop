Privacy Policy

Arrow Pop is a puzzle game for Android made by Praveena, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

No account, no personal details

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress, coins, stars and settings (such as sound, vibration and theme) on your device only. We cannot see this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

If the game ever crashes, a short technical report is saved on your phone so you can choose to copy it and send it to us. Nothing is sent automatically.

Reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can show a friendly reminder when you have not played for a while. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no information is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Pop is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Arrow Pop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
